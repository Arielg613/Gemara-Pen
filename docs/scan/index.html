    How it works: photograph the main Gemara text (not Rashi/Tosafot). The scanner reads it roughly,
    then matches it against the full Berakhot text stored on this device to find exactly where you are.
    Tap a word to see its meaning.


    Point at a word: in the camera, put the red laser line just under a word. With Live on, the meaning appears on
    screen by itself as you move. Or press Capture and it opens straight to that word.


    Meaning here the phrase you tapped and what it means in this exact spot, written by AI and checked against Sefaria's English (so far Berakhot 2a–7a and 46a).

    General meaning everywhere else for now: one meaning per word for the whole tractate, so it can be wrong for a particular spot.

    Official Sefaria's translation of the line (William Davidson / Steinsaltz). Bold = literal translation, plain = explanation.


    Offline: once the top corner says ✓ Ready offline, everything works with Wi-Fi off.
    On iPhone/iPad: Share → Add to Home Screen, then open the Home Screen icon once with Wi-Fi on and wait
    for “✓ Ready offline” — the Home Screen app keeps its own separate copy from Safari.
